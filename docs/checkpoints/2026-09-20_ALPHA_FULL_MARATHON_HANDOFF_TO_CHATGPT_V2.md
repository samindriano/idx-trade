Prior V2/V3/V4/V4-X1, ranking, price-path, participation, regime, financial,
foreign-flow, ownership, and abandoned/orphan directions were mapped into
separate classes:

- engineering/conformance failure;
- source or PIT admission block;
- sparse or partial capability;
- structural overlap caution;
- economics/capacity caution;
- semantically invalid source interpretation;
- genuinely untested family.

This distinction matters: “not admitted,” “not tested,” and “failed as written”
are not interchangeable. Later PIT/integrity adjudications supersede older
promotion headlines where the evidence changed.
