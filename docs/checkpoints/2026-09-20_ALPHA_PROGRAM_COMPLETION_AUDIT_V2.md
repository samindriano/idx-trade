## Remaining blockers and reopening evidence

- Eligibility: hash-bound project authority selecting exact semantics, followed
  by isolated regeneration/replay.
- Population/PIT/survivorship: daily PIT universe with delisted/relisted,
  ticker-reuse, and issuer/security continuity.
- CA/basis: population-complete event-to-window, effective/knowledge-time, and
  adjusted/unadjusted basis authority.
- Revision/vintage: publication/available-at metadata for financial/flow data.
- Capacity: PIT ADV, spread, queue, fill, and executable capacity evidence.
- Tooling adoption: independent review and explicit adoption of nested schema
  and versioned-result contracts.
- Predictive evaluation: only after all admission gates pass and one-shot
  protected evaluation is explicitly authorized.
