## Why another local experiment is low information

The committed completion audit and current coordination state agree that:

- Data QA is blocked on population completeness and historical as-of authority;
- the 100-session prospective evaluation is blocked at `2/100` with no
  admitted inventory or sealed target attestation;
- Decision V2 research is closed;
- public sources remain discovery/event-level rather than PIT authority;
- structural reruns, monotone transforms, public ticker snapshots, and
  parameter tuning would repeat already-closed evidence.

The current frontier therefore requires one or more of:

1. population-complete PIT membership and survivorship/lifecycle authority;
2. issuer/ISIN continuity and event-complete corporate-action/price-basis
   lineage;
3. publication/revision/available-at authority for financial and market data;
4. historical executable ADV/spread/queue/fill/capacity evidence;
5. an authorized future protected evaluation under the frozen packet;
6. external production evidence from the live EOD/E2E lane.

None of these can be manufactured safely from the current local panel or by
repeating another target-free candidate experiment.
