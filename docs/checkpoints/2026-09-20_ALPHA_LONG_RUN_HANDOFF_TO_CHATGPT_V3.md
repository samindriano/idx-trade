  of the current eligible rows; 277 dates with at least 30 names; daily max
  175; median 0 because C3 is sparse.

The latest history audit changes the interpretation of the conflict:

- commit `26aac816` contains `PRIMARY_LIQUIDITY_LOOKBACK = 60` and
  `PRIMARY_MIN_ACTIVE_OBSERVATIONS = 20`, manually filtering finite ACTIVE
  observations within official-session windows;
- the same ref's specification states at least 20 valid ACTIVE observations in
  a trailing 60-session window;
- the later 2026-09-19 protocol repeats the wording but is not its origin;
- Stage-A V1 uses complete-window rolling but no explicit later >=20 mask;
- Stage-A V2 adds >=20 after complete-window rolling, making that check
  redundant for the implementation's count/median fields;
- `security_master` has a separate 60-session / `IPO_WARMUP` concept.

This narrows provenance but does not authorize the policy. Do not choose a
branch by code majority, sample size, perceived conservatism, structural
metrics, or convenience.

## 8. What would legitimately reopen the project

The highest-value missing evidence is:

1. population-complete PIT universe with delisted/relisted/ticker-reuse and
   issuer/ISIN continuity;
2. population-complete corporate-action event-to-window and
   adjusted/unadjusted basis authority with effective and knowledge time;
3. publication-time and revision/vintage authority for financial and flow
   sources;
4. historical executable ADV, spread, queue, fill, and capacity data;
5. a hash-bound eligibility policy decision that explicitly binds the
   20-in-60 legacy contract or the complete-window interpretation to this new
   protocol;

More rows, a larger provider, nicer structural statistics, or another model
variant alone do not reopen the gates.
