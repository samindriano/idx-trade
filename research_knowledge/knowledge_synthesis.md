# Knowledge Synthesis

## What we know

- The corrected structural C1–C4 implementation is reproducible on the frozen
  panel under its current contract.
- C1/C2/C4 are structurally broad enough for future research; C3 is sparse and
  late under the current financial bundle.
- Monotone ranking transforms are redundant; C1/C4 have material structural
  dependence; combinations can be measured structurally but are not new alpha IDs.
- H-LIQ/H-VOL/H-EXC-02 are bounded research hypotheses, not predictive winners.
- Source, CA, identity, PIT, survivorship, revision, and executable capacity
  authority are separate gates and remain incomplete.
- Constructor, packet, firewall, and lane verifiers provide scoped reproducibility
  and process evidence, not scientific validity.
- Eligibility provenance is partially clarified: the protocol says 60 is the
  trailing official-session lookback and 20 is the minimum finite observations;
  the generic security-master warm-up path is separate. Both Stage-A versions
  inherit `min_periods=window`, so rolling completeness appears to have been
  conflated with the final eligibility mask. This does not select the policy.

## What we think we know

- C1 is more fragile than C2/C4 under the registered unresolved price-basis
  stress, but this is a risk signal rather than a corrected-data result.
- H-LIQ-01 has a distinct structural surface but can become participation- and
  regime-dependent in the top-value bucket.
- Lower proxy turnover does not imply executable capacity or profitability.
- Broad local source search is now low-value unless a new source contract appears.

## What failed, is redundant, or is superseded

- Initial Stage-A v1: implementation failure.
- Monotone rank/z/robust-z variants: redundant.
- Raw H-EXC-01: representation failure as written.
- Same-surface H-LIQ residual retry: no-go without orthogonal evidence.
- Exact prior formulations and semantically unsupported margin interpretations:
  no-retry at the tested representation/source scope.
- Earlier positional robustness metrics: superseded by key-aligned results.

## What remains unknown or blocked

- `POLICY_AUTHORITY_MISSING` for min-periods semantics.
- Population completeness, historical PIT/available-at time, survivorship,
  issuer/ISIN continuity, CA basis, revision/vintage, common support, and
  executable capacity.
- Every predictive question: IC, ICIR, OOS, PnL, incumbent comparison, and
  prospective evidence.

## What would change our mind

- A hash-bound eligibility authority record and authorized isolated regeneration.
- Population-complete PIT/identity/CA/revision admission for the relevant feature
  family and common support.
- New source evidence with publication/knowledge time and semantics, not merely
  more rows or a larger sample.
- A genuine non-redundant mechanism with a pre-registered admission path.

## Highest-value missing data

1. Historical point-in-time population/universe with delisted/relisted/ticker-
   reuse and issuer/ISIN continuity.
2. Population-complete corporate-action event-to-window and adjusted/unadjusted
   basis authority with effective and knowledge time.
3. Revision/vintage/publication-time authority for financial and flow sources.
4. Historical executable liquidity: ADV, spread, queue, fill, and capacity.
5. Only after those: protected common-support H5/H10 outcome adjudication.

## Next researcher rule

Do not turn structural success into predictive claims. Do not choose the
eligibility population by convenience. Use the registries and reopen conditions
before starting any old experiment again.
