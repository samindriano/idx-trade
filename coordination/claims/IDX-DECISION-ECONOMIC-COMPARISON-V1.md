# IDX Decision Economic Comparison V1

Status: ACTIVE

Purpose: one consolidated, non-tuning economic comparison of naive daily Top10 versus frozen Decision V1/V2/V3 on the already-used 600-session Decision development window.

Scope: reuse frozen policy target/holding ledgers and the accepted execution-price/corporate-action lineage; reconstruct next-session-open implementation and compare gross/net portfolio outcomes under a fixed transaction-cost sensitivity grid.

Boundaries: no Decision V4 implementation, no policy threshold tuning, no alpha refit/retune, no protected/fresh-forward access, no provider/network calls, no paper/live activation. Results are development evidence only, not untouched final validation.
